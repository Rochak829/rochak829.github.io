About

Academic and research background.

I completed my Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering at the National Institute of Technology, Jalandhar, India in 2021. From 2021 to 2025, I worked at Maruti Suzuki R&D, where I was involved in the design and development of fuel tank and CNG parts.

I am currently working in the Laboratory for Innovations in Sensing, Estimation, and Control under Professor Rajesh Rajamani at the University of Minnesota. My interests sit at the intersection of controls, mechatronics, sensing, and mechanical system development.
