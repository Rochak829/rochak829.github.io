About

Academic and research background.

Rochak completed his Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering at the National Institute of Technology, Jalandhar, India in 2021. From 2021 to 2025, he worked at Maruti Suzuki R&D on fuel-tank systems for the Dzire compact sedan and Swift hatchback, from concept and development through mass production. He also contributed to development work for the Eeco multipurpose van and Super Carry light commercial mini-truck, including work supporting the upcoming BS6 Phase 3 / AIS-175 emissions requirements.

He is currently working as a Research Assistant in the Laboratory for Innovations in Sensing, Estimation and Control under Professor Rajesh Rajamani at the University of Minnesota, developing state estimation algorithms for agricultural robots.
